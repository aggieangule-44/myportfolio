01 / About me

Beyond the numbers: my journey towards meaningful impact.

My journey began in Kenya, where I developed a deep appreciation for education, community-led development and access to opportunity.

With an academic foundation in Biostatistics, I began exploring how data could inform decisions, strengthen programmes and improve people's lives. My experiences across research, monitoring and evaluation, public institutions and international development revealed an important lesson: behind every statistic is a human story.

As a Mastercard Foundation Scholar at the University of Edinburgh, I pursued an MSc in Data, Inequality and Society, deepening my understanding of the relationships between data, power, technology and social justice.

Today, my work brings together research, evidence-informed decision-making, entrepreneurship and social innovation. As founder of ClimateClan360 and co-founder of Kakamega Women Telecenter, I am particularly interested in creating opportunities for women, young people and underserved communities through education, technology and sustainable development.

My professional journey across Kenya and the United Kingdom continues to shape my global perspective and commitment to inclusive innovation.

My vision for the future

My ambition is to contribute to a world where data and technology are instruments of inclusion rather than sources of inequality. Through research, public service, entrepreneurship and international collaboration, I aim to advance evidence-informed policies, responsible innovation, climate resilience and equitable access to opportunity.

I aspire to bridge research and implementation, connecting global innovation ecosystems with local communities and developing sustainable solutions that respond to real human needs.
