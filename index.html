01 / About me

Turning evidence into meaningful action.

I am a research, data and social impact professional with a background in biostatistics, monitoring, evaluation and learning, and interdisciplinary research. My work spans international development, gender equality, digital inclusion and climate education. As founder of ClimateClan360, I am exploring how technology and gamification can help young people translate climate knowledge into action.

My approach brings together rigorous evidence, community perspectives and practical innovation, with a commitment to ensuring that the people most affected by development decisions have a voice in shaping them.
